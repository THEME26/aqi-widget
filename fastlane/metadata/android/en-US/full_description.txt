AQI Bar keeps the current air quality where you can see it: four home-screen widgets in different sizes, and one quiet line in the status bar.

WHAT YOU GET
- Four widget sizes: 4x2 (value, level, gauge, station, dominant pollutant), 2x1 (gauge, value, action), 1x2 (vertical) and 1x1 (colour and number only).
- All widgets read the same cached reading, so the number, the colour and the ring never disagree.
- A silent, persistent notification. The icon is a face, not a number: the status bar only keeps a silhouette, and six levels of fill are indistinguishable at 24 dp.
- On Android 16 and later the notification can become a system Live Update on the lock screen and the always-on display. The system draws it; the app runs no foreground service and never wakes the screen.

TWO AQI SCALES, COMPUTED ON THE DEVICE
- US AQI with the 2024 EPA breakpoints, from raw concentrations of PM2.5, PM10, ozone, nitrogen dioxide, sulphur dioxide and carbon monoxide.
- China AQI (HJ 633-2012) from the same raw values.
- Averaging windows follow the breakpoint tables, not the instantaneous peak.

AREA CONTEXT
One station is not the whole district. The app samples nine grid points around you at 10, 25 or 50 km and reports either the median or the worst cell.

DATA SOURCES
- Open-Meteo (default): global model data, no key, no registration.
- WAQI: station-level readings, optional, needs a free token from the World Air Quality Index Project.
Both can be shown side by side; a missing key shows a placeholder instead of quietly borrowing the other source's number.

HONEST ABOUT STALE DATA
When the last successful refresh is older than twice the refresh interval, the card turns neutral grey and keeps the number, because the number is still a real observation. Grey never means a request failed.

PRIVACY
- Location is used to get coordinates for the request. It is not stored and goes nowhere else.
- A WAQI token stays in private app storage, excluded from cloud backup.
- No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, no account.
- The only network traffic is the request to the source you selected.

LANGUAGES
Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, Spanish, German.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. Free software under the GNU GPL v3.
