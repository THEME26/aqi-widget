New application id, so this version installs alongside 1.0.x. Remove the old app once the reading shows up again.

- Cards no longer turn grey after a single failed request. Grey now describes only the age of the reading, as it was meant to.
- Scheduled refreshes wait for a network connection instead of failing while the radio is still waking up.
- A failed refresh retries with backoff instead of being reported as a success.
- Release builds are signed with a dedicated key and are no longer debuggable.
- Source released under the GNU GPL v3.
